# GCSE Maths reference

## Number
- **Fractions:** to add or subtract, rewrite both fractions over a common denominator, then add or subtract the numerators only. To multiply, multiply tops and bottoms. To divide, multiply by the reciprocal of the second fraction.
- **Percentages:** a percentage is a number out of 100. Use a multiplier: an increase of 15% is ×1.15, a decrease of 15% is ×0.85. Compound interest repeats the multiplier each year: amount × multiplier^years.
- **Ratio:** to share in a ratio, add the parts, divide the total by the number of parts to find one part, then multiply.
- **Indices:** a^m × a^n = a^(m+n); a^m ÷ a^n = a^(m−n); (a^m)^n = a^(mn); a^0 = 1; a^−n = 1/a^n.
- **Standard form:** A × 10^n where 1 ≤ A < 10 and n is an integer.

## Algebra
- **Expanding brackets:** multiply every term inside by the term outside; for two brackets multiply every term by every term.
- **Factorising:** take out the highest common factor; quadratics x² + bx + c factorise to (x + p)(x + q) where p + q = b and pq = c.
- **Linear equations:** do the same inverse operation to both sides until the unknown is on its own.
- **Simultaneous equations:** make the coefficients of one unknown match, then add or subtract to eliminate it, or substitute one equation into the other.
- **Quadratic equations:** factorise and set each bracket to zero, or use x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / 2a.
- **Sequences:** an arithmetic sequence has nth term dn + (first term − d), where d is the common difference.
- **Straight lines:** y = mx + c, where m is the gradient (change in y ÷ change in x) and c is the y-intercept.

## Geometry
- **Pythagoras:** only in right-angled triangles, a² + b² = c², where c is the hypotenuse, the longest side opposite the right angle.
- **Trigonometry:** in right-angled triangles, sin = opposite/hypotenuse, cos = adjacent/hypotenuse, tan = opposite/adjacent (SOH CAH TOA).
- **Area:** rectangle = length × width; triangle = ½ × base × height; trapezium = ½(a + b)h; parallelogram = base × height.
- **Circles:** circumference = πd = 2πr; area = πr².
- **Volume:** prism = area of cross-section × length; cylinder = πr²h.
- **Angles:** angles on a straight line add to 180°, around a point to 360°, in a triangle to 180°, in a quadrilateral to 360°. Interior angles of an n-sided polygon add to (n − 2) × 180°. Alternate angles are equal, corresponding angles are equal, co-interior angles add to 180°.

## Probability and statistics
- **Probability:** always between 0 and 1. P(not A) = 1 − P(A). For independent events multiply probabilities; for mutually exclusive events add them.
- **Averages:** mean = total ÷ number of values; median = middle value when ordered; mode = most common value; range = largest − smallest.
